FAQ

Good questions

Anything else? Message the clinic team on WhatsApp and a person replies.

Can I see a doctor today?

Same-day slots open every morning for both clinic and video visits. The booking calendar only shows times that are still free, so what you pick is what you get.

How do video consultations work?

You get a secure link by SMS and WhatsApp before your visit. It opens in your phone’s browser, so there is no app to install. If your connection drops, the doctor calls you back on your phone.

Do you accept HMOs?

Add your HMO and membership number when you book, and the clinic confirms your cover before the visit. You can always choose to pay yourself instead.

What happens after my consultation?

You get a written summary, any prescriptions, and a follow-up date. Your doctor checks in on that date by message or call, and you can reply with questions in the meantime.

Is this for emergencies?

No. For chest pain, severe bleeding, difficulty breathing or any emergency, call 112 or go straight to the nearest emergency unit.
